Generating phone enquiries

A separate call-focused campaign recorded 21 calls placed at ₹43.94 per call, with ₹922.71 in reported spend.

21CALLS PLACED
₹43.94COST PER CALL

Call campaign snapshotREPORT CONTEXT

Performance snapshot

The report records 21 calls placed at ₹43.94 per call, with ₹922.71 spent.

The objective

This campaign used calls as the primary result. The key question for this type of activity is not only how many calls were placed, but whether those calls connected with the right customers.

Approach & learnings

Keep the call objective distinct from messaging campaigns and review call quality alongside cost metrics.

What the numbers say

  • Present the supplied report metrics without combining separate campaigns or content.
  • Use the result metric that matches the campaign objective.
  • Review lead quality or business outcomes separately when that data is available.

Next step

For a stronger published case study, add the original dashboard screenshot and the confirmed reporting period. That makes the work easier for a potential client to verify.

Data note: Call count is presented exactly as shown in the supplied report; connected calls or sales are not inferred.
