THE THINKING

03 / THE APPROACH

Marketing isn't guesswork.
It starts with better questions.

Before deciding what to post, which ads to run, or where to invest, I look at:

  • Who are we trying to reach?
  • Why should they choose this business?
  • What's stopping them from taking action?
  • Which channel makes sense for this goal?
  • How will we know if the strategy is working?

The right marketing move starts with understanding the actual problem.
