More messaging conversations

A Meta messaging campaign generated 108 messaging conversations at ₹4.76 per conversation, with ₹513.75 in reported spend.

Performance snapshot

The reported campaign snapshot shows 108 messaging conversations at an average cost of ₹4.76 per conversation. Total reported spend was ₹513.75.

The objective

The objective was to encourage potential customers to start a conversation through messaging. This page documents the results visible in the campaign report, without assuming that every conversation became a qualified lead or sale.

Approach & learnings

Focus the campaign around the messaging objective, monitor the cost per result, and review the conversations themselves to understand lead quality.

What the numbers say

  • Present the supplied report metrics without combining separate campaigns or content.
  • Use the result metric that matches the campaign objective.
  • Review lead quality or business outcomes separately when that data is available.

Next step

For a stronger published case study, add the original dashboard screenshot and the confirmed reporting period. That makes the work easier for a potential client to verify.

Data note: This is one separate campaign report. Dates and attribution details should be confirmed against the original Meta Ads export before publication.
