Performance snapshot

The snapshot shows 4.35K clicks from 37.2K impressions, with an 11.7% CTR and average position of 15.3.

The objective

This case study focuses on organic search visibility. Search Console metrics help show how often pages appeared in search, how often users clicked, and the average position recorded for the report.

Approach & learnings

Use search queries and page-level data to identify opportunities for better relevance, stronger titles and descriptions, and improved content coverage.

What the numbers say

  • Present the supplied report metrics without combining separate campaigns or content.
  • Use the result metric that matches the campaign objective.
  • Review lead quality or business outcomes separately when that data is available.

Next step

For a stronger published case study, add the original dashboard screenshot and the confirmed reporting period. That makes the work easier for a potential client to verify.

Data note: The exact report date range should be added from the original Search Console export before publishing.
