The person behind the strategy

I care about what marketing does for a business.

I'm Afnan, a digital marketer who works across social media, paid campaigns and SEO to help businesses communicate clearly and make better marketing decisions.

I like to start with the real problem: Are the right people discovering the business? Do they understand the offer? Is the content building trust? Are campaigns being measured against the right outcome?

From there, I turn the answers into a practical plan — then learn from the results instead of relying on guesswork.
